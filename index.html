About

I am a certified mobile phlebotomist dedicated to providing safe, reliable, and convenient on-site blood collection services directly to your doorstep. With a strong focus on patient care, I strive to create a comfortable and stress-free experience. Whether visiting patients in their homes, workplaces, or care facilities, I ensure each procedure is performed with precision, professionalism, and compassion. My goal is to make essential lab services more accessible while maintaining the highest standards of care and confidentiality.

Services

My services include routine blood draws, diagnostic specimen collection, and support for lab testing as ordered by your healthcare provider. I work with individuals, families, medical offices, and care facilities to ensure each sample is collected safely, handled properly, and delivered promptly to the appropriate laboratory based on your insurance provider’s requirements for accurate results.
